อู่ซ่อมหุ่นยนต์ใต้ทะเล / Deep Sea Robot Repair — Kairon Arcade (ห้องเกม ไครอน)

Code: original, written for Kairon Arcade. No third-party code or libraries.
Art: every robot, the mechanic, the garage, the floors and the effects are drawn in code (HTML5 canvas) by our own script. No image assets except the Kairon Arcade logo.
Music & sound effects: original, synthesised procedurally in Python (numpy) by our own script. No samples.
Voice lines (Thai): generated with VachanaTTS voice "th_f_2" (VIZINTZOR/VachanaTTS on Hugging Face, model license: MIT), then pitch-shifted by our own ffmpeg script. MIT permits commercial use.
