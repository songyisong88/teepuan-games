ตลาดน้ำอวกาศ / Star River Market — Kairon Arcade (ห้องเกม ไครอน)

Code: original, written for Kairon Arcade. No third-party code or libraries.
Art: original sprites generated for this game (AI image generation from our own concept), cut out and converted by our own script.
Music & sound effects: original, synthesised procedurally in Python (numpy) by our own script. No samples.
Voice lines (Thai): generated with VachanaTTS voice "th_f_2" (VIZINTZOR/VachanaTTS on Hugging Face, model license: MIT),
  then pitch-shifted by our own ffmpeg script. MIT permits commercial use.
Font: Kanit by Cadson Demak — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (fonts/OFL-Kanit.txt), self-hosted.

Travel update (v2): the new art (riverless space backdrop, floating islets, Fang portrait, pointer hand), the new sounds (paddle loop, dock chime, combo, gift, set-off, fanfare) and the new Thai voice lines were made the same way as above.

Candy Planet update (stage 2): the Candy Planet backdrop, candy islets and stalls, three new aliens, four new sweets, Fang hats, boat colours and icons were generated and cut out the same way (boat colours are recoloured from our own boat sprite). The Candy Planet music, quest and shop sounds are procedural like the rest, and the new Thai voice lines use the same VachanaTTS voice and processing.

Glow Ice update (stage 3): the Glow Ice Planet backdrop, ice islets and stall, four new aliens, four new frozen goods, ice-block / meteor / crate obstacles, the three pets, the home-port market stalls and the new icons were generated from our own concepts and cut out by our own script, the same way as above. The ice music and the new sounds (lane swish, soft bump, crate grab, stall build, pet chirp, meteor shower, order chime) are procedural like the rest. The new Thai voice lines use the same VachanaTTS "th_f_2" voice (MIT) and the same pitch-up processing.

Emerald Forest update (stage 4): the Emerald Forest backdrop, forest islets and stall, four new aliens (and their golden sticker look, tinted in code), four forest goods, log / thorn obstacles, the fork sign, fireflies, the Forest Bunny pet, the leaf hat, the three boat classes (paddle boat, long-tail boat, floating-market raft) with their colour variants, and the new icons (book, gift, jar, lure) were generated from our own concepts and cut out by our own script, the same way as above. The forest music and the new sounds (fork chime, firefly catch, night fall, sticker, page complete, golden alien, daily gift, boat upgrade) are procedural like the rest. The new Thai voice lines use the same VachanaTTS "th_f_2" voice (MIT) and the same pitch-up processing (no other TTS engine).
